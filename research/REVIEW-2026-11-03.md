## What the baseline audit changes

The [real-engine empty-record audit](model-improvements/baseline-audit.md) gives **58/96** on the original
AMI split, against the October 4B ledger's **59/96**. Only 38 questions need a value or real change:
the ledger answers 18, full context 26 and BM25 23. Perfect review and the DeepSeek extractor each answer 31.
The historical comparisons are preserved, with this qualification in the public summary and private report.

The paired tests are question-level, exploratory and do not account for dependence within a meeting series.
A non-significant test does not show equivalence. Source audits are also needed: incomplete gold can
under-credit valid decisions, while broad evidence windows can over-credit a wrong attribution.
