The problem

Meetings make decisions. Then the decisions drift.

Which decision holds? A recap of the last meeting might say Alpha Systems. The record says Beta Cloud and keeps Alpha Systems as history. You can inspect, edit, accept or reject proposed values.

Conversation reshapes what groups remember: a selective recap slows recall of what it left out (Coman, Manier and Hirst, 2009). Talking can also help later recall (Marion and Thorley, 2016).

Details: the evidence, rated
  • Moderate

    19 → 7

    People search past meetings when colleagues remember differently. 12 faculty described 19 times they needed details from meetings they had attended; 7 were about a decision, and several of those began with conflicting recollections. Small academic sample.

    Banerjee, Rose and Rudnicky, INTERACT 2005

  • Strong

    d = .59

    Talking reshapes what groups remember, and can also help. A meta-analysis found better later individual recall after collaboration (22 studies), alongside the costs of recalling together. So: reshapes, not destroys.

    Marion and Thorley, Psychological Bulletin 2016

  • Strong

    N = 308

    Review can fail too. In a preregistered study, explanations raised reliance on both correct and incorrect AI answers; clickable sources lowered reliance on wrong ones. So Memoria shows the quote, and review accuracy has to be measured.

    Kim et al., CHI 2025

Supporting literature (4 more findings)
  • Strong Selective recaps slow recall of what was left out. When one person recaps selectively, listeners later have slower access to related details that were not mentioned. Coman, Manier and Hirst, 2009; Cuc, Koppel and Hirst, 2007.
  • Moderate AI meeting notes make factual and speaker errors. In a 2024 benchmark, a GPT-3.5 setup had hallucinations in 5 of 35 meeting summaries and wrong-reference errors in 3 of 35. An older model: not a rate for today's products. Kirstein et al., Findings of EMNLP 2024.
  • Strong Recording meetings raises consent questions. In In re Otter.AI Privacy Litigation, a 13 Aug 2026 order let core claims proceed; that is not a finding of liability. A separate case against Fireflies was voluntarily dismissed on 11 Mar 2026. N.D. Cal. No. 5:25-cv-06911; C.D. Ill. No. 3:25-cv-03399.
  • Moderate The real rival is good minutes and a simple log. Public minutes already work as negotiated records of decisions. Memoria has to show it does better on accuracy and effort; that comparison has not been run yet. Whittaker, Laban and Tucker, 2006.

Ratings come from our desk review of 44 sources, accessed 4 Nov 2026. Strong does not mean universal. Left out on purpose: the claim that drift causes costly rework, which the evidence rates Weak.

Propose. Approve. Remember.

The model proposes a change with its source quote. A person approves first values and changes. The record keeps earlier values with their sources.

Details: the contract, precisely

The contract, precisely

Code locates source text and stores the quotation copied from it. First values and value changes wait for approval; unchanged confirmations may record automatically. Original claims and evidence are retained; old intervals are closed. Manual corrections show the reviewer, reason and time. These checks establish provenance, not that the quotation supports the value.

Method detail: the model's quote is matched to the transcript ignoring case and spacing. If that fails and the quote has at least 3 words, the turn that covers at least 80% of its words, in order, is cited, and that whole turn is stored.

Where the analogy stops

No parallel branches to merge, and no cryptographic hashes. Original claims and quotes are retained; an old value is closed by setting its end time. Unchanged confirmations may record without review.

Versioned memory is not new

MoM, TEM and Zep keep versions and history; EvoWiki versions meeting knowledge; two-date tables come from temporal databases. What we did not find, in a scan on 4 Nov 2026, is this combination for spoken meetings. Related work

Engineering

Language models are good at reading and bad at keeping state.

Ledgers are good at state. So the model proposes, a person approves, and the ledger remembers.

3 resident workers (speech, language, embeddings) run pretrained models on the Mac over local sockets; approvals change records, not model weights.

Ask the ledger, not the model.

  1. Tracked values are looked up in the ledger, not generated.
  2. History questions use labelled versions0.3.5-r2, after TEM (arXiv 2610.21900): each version reaches the model labelled current, replaced or corrected.
  3. Other questions are drafted from approved passages, with checked citations.

Details: what this does not show
  • The local extractor is weak.On the laptop's 4B with no review: 59/96 against 58/96 for an empty record. On the 38 questions needing a recorded value or a nonzero change count: 18/38, against 26/38 for full transcripts and 23/38 for BM25 search.
  • Review has a cost.Reviewing every proposed change means about 4.6 suggestions per meeting, in a replay of historical candidates.
  • Human review is not yet measured.Its accuracy and effort are unknown. The user test is pending: 0 participant sessions so far.
  • Approval is not agreement.A quote shows wording in the stored transcript; it does not establish an agreed decision. An approval is an action, not proof that the group agreed.
  • One known gap.Known defect, found 6 Nov in review: an automatic same-value confirmation can cancel an approved future-dated change, or replace the quote and date an answer cites, because the gate checks the value only at the confirmation's start time. Fix designed and staged with tests (it compares the whole timeline and the cited sources before recording), not built. The staged fix is version 2, with 26 new tests.
  • Narrow scope.Single user, Apple-silicon Mac only, no calendar or CRM integrations. Real-speech tests are English only; tracked items are defined by the user first.
  • Evaluation data.AMI meetings are role-played design meetings. The stress-test series were written by AI agents. AI agents designed, ran and scored the overnight studies; human adjudication is pending. The author is responsible for the work.
  • Privacy scope.One run of about 2 minutes. File import, briefs and long sessions were not measured. Optional inherited features, such as Git remotes or external AI providers, can use the network if a user turns them on.
  • Builds.0.3.4 is installed. 0.3.6, the latest build, is built, software checks passed, not installed; it adds Quick Ask and the hang guard to 0.3.5-r2. Keeping every version also needs a retention and deletion policy.

Prior art

Related work.

Memoria builds on temporal databases (Jensen and Snodgrass, 1999), MoM (arXiv 2609.25054), TEM (arXiv 2610.21900), Zep (arXiv 2501.13956), EvoWiki (arXiv 2608.23265), Suggested Is Not Superseded (arXiv 2610.15710) and work on decisions in dialogue.

Details: what each one does, and what overlaps
  • Temporal databasesJensen and Snodgrass, IEEE TKDE 1999

    Valid time and transaction time: the two dates Memoria stores for every value. doi:10.1109/69.755613

  • MoM: Memory of MemoryQin and Lu, arXiv 2609.25054, 2026

    The closest prior art: one current value per key, displaced values kept as provenance. MoM commits on arrival; in Memoria you can inspect, edit, accept or reject proposed values. arXiv 2609.25054

  • TEMSong, Zhuang and Shi, arXiv 2610.21900, 2026

    Versioned, source-linked facts with history labels; Memoria 0.3.5's history-labelled Ask uses this idea. arXiv 2610.21900

  • ZepRasmussen et al., arXiv 2501.13956, 2025

    A temporal knowledge graph for agent memory that updates automatically; Memoria is local and asks a person before a value changes. arXiv 2501.13956

  • EvoWikiChen, Wang and Que, arXiv 2608.23265, 2026

    The closest meeting work: versioned entity records with evidence spans. We found no past-date record questions, human confirmation, speaker attribution or on-device operation in it. arXiv 2608.23265

  • Suggested Is Not SupersededChen and Le, arXiv 2610.15710, 2026

    A yes/no model gate retired 39.2% of claims whose change was only suggested; the paper recommends routing them to pending. Memoria's pending default does this, with a person as the gate. arXiv 2610.15710

  • Decisions in dialogueFernández et al., 2008; Purver et al., 2007

    Detecting decisions in multi-party meetings is hard; extraction is our weak step too. ACL Anthology W08-0125

What we did not find, in a short scan on 4 Nov 2026: this full combination for spoken meetings, with typed decisions and copied quotes, proposed values you can inspect, edit, accept or reject, two time views, a live replaced-decision cue, on a 16 GB laptop. A short scan cannot prove absence.
