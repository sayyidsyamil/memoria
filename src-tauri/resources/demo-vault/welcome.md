# Welcome to Memoria

Memoria keeps the decisions and promises from your meetings, and the exact words that prove each one. Everything stays on this computer.

This vault already contains three short, fictional meetings of a "Digital Procurement Pilot" steering committee (Aisha, Raj and Wei Ling). Use them to try the whole flow in about ten minutes.

## Try it in five steps

1. Open **Decisions & commitments** (sidebar, or the note-list menu, or the command palette).
2. Go to the **Setup** tab and press **Load sample setup**. This tells Memoria which things to track: the vendor, the budget, the go-live date, and three promises (training, data migration, security review).
3. Go to the **Meetings** tab. The three meeting notes are listed. Press **Add** on each one, oldest first, or press **Add all new, oldest first**. The first time can take a few minutes because Memoria reads the meeting on this computer.
4. Look at **Now** to see what is currently decided. Press **Evidence** on any row to see the exact quote and who said it. Anything Memoria is unsure about waits in **To review** until you accept, edit or reject it.
5. Go to **Ask** and try "What is the current go-live date?" or "How many times did the go-live date change?". Every answer shows its evidence. Use **Prepare** to get the open commitments and recent changes for your next meeting.

## Use it with your own meetings

Use **Record meeting** to transcribe a live meeting, or save any transcript note. Then add it under **Meetings** in the same way.

## Good to know

- Nothing is sent over the internet. Voice recognition of people is off unless you turn it on in Settings, and you can delete saved voice profiles at any time.
- Corrections are kept: if you fix a value, the earlier value stays in its history.
